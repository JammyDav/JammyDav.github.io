Training Store

Search an invented catalogue of laboratory training. Find training by course name, or by the equipment it covers: search a manufacturer, model or equipment class (try GLE, chromatography or the made-up model __EXAMPLE_MODEL__), or tick the filters on the left. Use the Training and Validation tabs to switch between courses and curricula, and tests and observation checklists. Curricula are shown as whole programmes with their courses inside. Every title, manufacturer and model here is made up, and links go nowhere.
